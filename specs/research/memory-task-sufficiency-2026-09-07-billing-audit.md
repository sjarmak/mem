Its [2400,0] witness needs an allocation agreement, or the future oracle should compare only totals. The smallest discriminating next test holds guidance fixed across an omitted-scope consumer and a matched fully supplied consumer, after a real producer, with no seeded memories or deleted sources. Check source choice, correctness, capture fidelity and effort separately. Add permanent/current/historical/repetition stages after proving the input information distinction. A distinct seeded-record diagnostic may test routing when organic capture failed, but cannot count as an autonomous complete lifecycle.

Mandatory approved-policy capture plus policy preflight and a catalog is a defensible new intervention, not evidence the previous conditional rule was violated. The strongest flaw would be changing those cues and the corpus together without retaining old guidance: adoption gain would be causally ambiguous. Preflight must confirm approved scope rather than merely confirm code matches a saved note. Stable references must originate from actual captures; no guessed facts. No-save controls should request a one-off artifact under fully supplied existing behavior rather than create another permanent endpoint.

Human offloading experiments distinguish cognitive ability from choice of external support and vary reminder costs/benefits; inaccurate confidence partly affects choice. The useful transfer is experimental separation of knowledge availability, perceived need and tool action—not a claim that LLMs share human mechanisms. Primary source: [Gilbert et al., Optimal use of reminders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31448938/).
